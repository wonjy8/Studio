# Google product artwork attribution

Downloaded 2026-10-24 from the official product asset URLs linked by
https://workspace.google.com/ (verified against its actual HTML). Original SVG bytes,
colors, geometry and embedded gradient IDs are retained; use as external images
rather than inlining repeated SVG IDs. Scale proportionally, without recoloring.

These are Google brand assets, **not** assets licensed by the repository's MIT,
Tabler, Ionicons or Simple Icons licenses. Google retains its rights in these
product marks. No open-source license is claimed for this artwork.

Applicable official guidance:

- https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/
- https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/use-cases/product-co-branding/
  (necessary product icons in compatible-product lists/product UI, accurate names,
  no endorsement implication, legal attribution in an appropriate product area)
- https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/branding
  (proportional resizing only; full Google Drive name; explanatory tooltips)
- https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/getting-started/brand-your-internal-communications-with-google-workspace
  (Google-approved artwork; do not distort or alter brand features)

Google Drive artwork was investigated but is not bundled: Studio does not currently
provide a Google Drive integration. Do not add an entry solely because an icon exists.

Use only next to the corresponding product name in supported connection management.
Availability of artwork does not establish a supported integration or OAuth grant.
Product icons are brand identification, not an outline/filled navigation state; do
not fabricate altered versions to follow the ordinary UI glyph pair convention.

Display attribution in an appropriate connection-management area:
“Google Docs, Google Forms, Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Google Sheets are
trademarks of Google LLC.”
